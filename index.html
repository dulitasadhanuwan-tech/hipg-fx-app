Financial Calculation & Integrity Rules

  • Official Daily Observations: Exact CBSL published observations retrieved directly from CBSL forms (exrates_results.php & exrates_resultstt.php).
  • No Synthetic Data / No Carry-Forward: Weekend and public holiday rates are strictly excluded from averaging. Daily gaps remain blank (Not Available).
  • Arithmetic Formula: All periodic rollups (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly) are computed strictly as:
    Average = SUM(actual_daily_observations) / n
  • Opening & Closing: Opening reflects the earliest valid observation date in the period; Closing reflects the latest. High & Low are exact extremes.
